People make things hoping to be the next Zucky that strikes gold. People depend on AI to teach them how to interact with others. And, people live their lives with curiosity as the breath that feeds the fire. I write to those — you.

All who have the patience to allow single streams of thought to channel through their mind like a laser beam in the night sky, it is rare in a world where everyone is so scatter-minded and fragile in their trains of thought. We all have a purpose for ourselves and for the world, but I think you are special. I'm betting that you are someone that sets themselves apart from convention. After all, who reads blogs anymore when AI can summarize it to you in a few lines. It's worth recognizing, the distinction between those who move through life as if it were goals and challenges, versus those who move through life as if it were abundances of fascinations.

Sometimes the line is blurred in that distinction, but for the sake of answering why I am making this — it's to inspire, untie, and unite the minds that are made for shaping the direction of our world. Direction. It is sometimes one of the least productive areas that we as a civilization can spend our time on — wasting away time with inaction. However, it is one of the greatest services we can do for our own fulfillment and evolution.
